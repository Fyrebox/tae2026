What you learned

    How SEO works (30 seconds)

    Search engines rank pages by mixing many signals. The biggest ones:

    • Content — does the page actually answer the query? Pillar posts and supporting articles build topical authority.
    • On-page — title tag, headings, schema markup, page speed.
    • Backlinks — other reputable sites linking to you. Quality > quantity. Cheap link buying can backfire.
    • Time — SEO compounds. Foundations (good content) make later actions (links) far more effective.

    In this game, you'll play one action per turn. Watch how prerequisites and risk interact.
