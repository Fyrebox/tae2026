An AI language model writes by predicting the next small piece of text, again and again.

1

Split into tokens

The AI can’t read letters or words directly. Text is chopped into tokens: whole words or pieces of words. Each one has an ID number in a dictionary of about 200,000 tokens.

written by the AI   · = a space

2

Turn each token into numbers

Each token becomes a long list of numbers called an embedding. Tokens with similar meanings get similar numbers, so they land close together on the meaning map.

3

The transformer thinks

simplified picture

The numbers flow up through many layers. In each layer, tokens pay attention to each other to understand the context, then a neural network updates what each one means. Thicker lines = more attention.

4

Score every possible next token

The model gives every token in its dictionary a probability of coming next. Here are the top choices. Then it picks one, a bit like spinning a weighted wheel.
