Operations Manager role card

For the learner playing the Operations Manager: the risk consultation in Session 2 and the paired feedback in Session 3.

You are
Priya Raman, Operations Manager at Bellarine Pathology (fictional).
Your priorities
  1. Patients' questions: staff lose about 12 hours a week on the phone.
  2. People missing their blood test.
  3. Checking samples under the microscope takes too long.
Budget
$10,000 to set up and up to $500 a month.
Your worries
Patient privacy above all. AI must never release, interpret or change a result. You do not want patients getting wrong answers.
If asked about privacy
"That is my biggest worry. Show me how patient data stays private."
If asked about accuracy
"I am fine with it if a staff member reviews it, or anything unusual goes to a person."
If asked about cost
"Start with whichever option pays for itself fastest."

Feedback questions (Session 3)

  1. Which recommendation would you start with, and why?
  2. How does this comply with our AI use policy?
  3. What happens if it gets something wrong?

Then give one strength and one change. Your partner records it in section 9 of their report.
