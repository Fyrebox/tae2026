Work brief

Organisation
Private pathology provider with 6 collection centres, one laboratory and about 40 staff.
Your role
ICT support officer, reporting to Priya Raman, Operations Manager.
Task
Identify opportunities to apply artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) that support Bellarine's goals. Recommend at least one AI, one ML and one DL opportunity.
Requested by
The Operations Manager. Confirm the goals with her before you start.
Organisational goals
  1. Reduce staff time spent on repetitive patient and GP enquiries.
  2. Reduce missed appointments.
  3. Speed up routine slide screening in the lab.
  4. Reduce specimen-labelling errors.
Existing technology
  • Booking system that sends an automatic SMS reminder 24 hours before every appointment
  • Microsoft 365: Outlook (with junk-mail filtering), Word, Teams; Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat available to staff (never with patient data)
  • Laboratory information system that flags out-of-range results
  • Barcode scanners at collection
Existing processes
  • Patient and GP enquiries are answered by phone and email by whoever is free
  • Routine slides are screened by eye by scientists
  • Missed appointments are tracked in a spreadsheet
Constraints
Set-up budget of $10,000 plus up to $500 a month. Must comply with the Privacy Act 1988, the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and the Bellarine AI use policy.
Deliverable
Bellarine AI Opportunities Report (online report form), with feedback from the Operations Manager acted on.
Due
End of Session 3.

Bellarine AI use policy (extract)

  • Only approved, business-tier AI tools may be used for work tasks.
  • Never enter patient or staff personal information into public AI tools.
  • AI must not release, interpret or change a pathology result. A scientist signs off every result.
  • AI-generated content sent to patients or GPs must be reviewed by staff.
  • Any new AI tool needs a documented risk assessment and Operations Manager approval before use.
  • AI errors or incidents must be reported to the Operations Manager.

Glossary

  • Fixed ruleA person wrote the rule. Same input, same output. Automation, not AI on its own.
  • Artificial intelligence (AI)Machines doing tasks that need human-like intelligence. For everyday tools, "did it learn from data?" is the useful test; the AI field also includes expert systems built from many expert rules.
  • Machine learning (ML)Learns patterns from examples instead of following fixed rules.
  • Deep learning (DL)Machine learning with many-layered neural networks, often for images, speech and text.
  • Large language model (LLM)Deep learning that predicts likely text (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini). It can sound sure and still be wrong.
  • Business-tier toolA paid, organisation-managed version with privacy and data protections.
